Original Games brings 30 games designed by our own studio into one app. Puzzles inspired by crafts such as kilim weaving, tile turning, marbling and calligraphy; mechanisms built from gears, mirrors and rail switches; logic games like the tulip garden, honeycomb and mushroom hunt; and small worlds such as the caravan route, the neighborhood and the aquarium. Sound Maze can be played without sight. Every game keeps its own progress, missions and achievements.

• Every day: Daily Trio
• Crafts: Kilim Weaving, Tile Master, Marbling Studio, Calligraphy Trace, Shadow Curtain, Paper Folding, Kundekari Panels, Illumination, Glass Bead Furnace, Potter's Wheel, Mosaic Restoration
• Folk tales: Hacivat and Karagöz, Nasreddin Hodja Tales
• Logic: Tulip Garden, Honeycomb Path, Mushroom Hunt, Market Stall, Constellations, Paint Mixer, Sound Maze
• Mechanisms: Gear Workshop, Light Path, Turning World, Toppling Tiles, Rail Switches, Hourglass
• Small worlds: Caravan Route, Build a Neighborhood, Aquarium Balance

Highlights
• 100,000 levels per game; every level is generated and checked against its rules to be solvable.
• Ten difficulty steps: relaxed early levels, expert challenges later.
• Hints reveal a correct step; undo and restart are always at hand.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
